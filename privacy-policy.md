# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CrescentEco ("CrescentEco," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the CrescentEco mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

**Information you provide to us.** When you create an account, request or schedule pest-control service, or contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, service and billing address, and details about the service you are requesting (for example, the type of pest issue or a description and photos of the affected area).

**Payment information.** When you pay for services through the App, your payment card and billing details are collected and processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store full payment card numbers on our own systems.

**Information collected automatically.** When you use the App, we automatically collect certain technical information, including your device type and operating system, unique device identifiers, app version, IP address, and usage information such as the features you interact with and the dates and times of your activity.

**Location information.** With your permission, we may collect approximate or precise location information from your device to help schedule service, confirm your service address, and improve routing. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings, though some features may not work as intended.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- create and manage your account;
- schedule, provide, and follow up on pest-control services;
- process payments and send receipts and invoices;
- communicate with you about appointments, service updates, and support requests;
- improve, personalize, and secure the App;
- detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or misuse; and
- comply with legal obligations.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below:

**Service providers.** We share information with vendors who perform services on our behalf, such as payment processing, hosting, analytics, and customer support, under obligations to protect your information.

**Field technicians.** We share the information necessary to fulfill your service request with the technician assigned to your appointment.

**Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

**Business transfers.** If CrescentEco is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond consistent with applicable law. You may also:

- update your account information within the App;
- adjust location and notification permissions in your device settings; and
- unsubscribe from marketing communications using the link provided in those messages.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

## 8. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may contain links to or integrations with third-party services. This Policy does not apply to those services, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
